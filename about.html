About

I design identities, then build everything they live on.

Jack Murray is a New York based brand designer who carries a project from the first sketch of a mark through to the films, the site, and the words on it.

Jack Murray is an entertainer, I just use design instead of song and dance. Every step in my process is built around the feelings that a project should evoke. Every project should start with a gasp, and build from there.

My experience at Parsons School of Design taught me that a designer must be a swiss army knife, prepared to do it all, or be the element a team needs to succeed.

My skills are formed from curiosity, always intrigued by could it be better? Can a 2D mark become a character, and live with you? Could a perfectly timed strum of a chord make a moment bigger?

What I make

Brand & Identity

Marks and systems

Logos, lockups, palette, and the rules that keep an identity intact across every application it has to survive.

Strategy & Naming

The argument first

Positioning, naming, and voice. The claim a brand is making before it makes a single mark.

Motion & 3D

Marks that move

Blender and After Effects. Identities built as objects rather than pictures, so a new format is a render instead of a commission.

Web Design & Build

Drawn, then built

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I design the site and then build it, paired with a headless CMS so a client can run it without code.

Type & Editorial

Hierarchy that holds

Typeface selection, specimens, and print. Hand-bound publications through to the body copy a brand ships every day.

Film & Photography

The brand somewhere real

Direction, cinematography, and editing. The footage that puts an identity in the world instead of on a swatch.
