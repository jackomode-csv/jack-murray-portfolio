The Creeping Body

A 50-page publication written, designed, and built during an intensive semester at Paris College of Art. I wrote all four essays, set the type, mapped the timelines, and printed every page myself, then hand bound the book and made its cover by hand, turning dense legal argument into something you can hold and actually read.
